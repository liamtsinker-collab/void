# Void — product spec (v0.1)

**Promise:** You don't need to figure out what you want by thinking. Try small things and let your own reactions show you.

**Audience:** 18+ at launch. (Under-18s need child-privacy compliance and extra safety design; separate version later.)

**Core loop (weekly):** Spin -> get an experiment -> do it -> 30-second reflection (energy before/after, want more / meh / never again, optional note) -> see your map of patterns.

**Fun principle:** If it isn't fun, nobody does it. The blob mascot literally fills with colour as you complete experiments. Spin animation, confetti, levels, badges. "Never again" still counts as a win.

**Small victories:** every completed experiment is a win regardless of outcome.

**Later:** pods of 3-5 people doing the same experiment (needs users first); reminders; a "doing it" timer; accounts; more experiments (target 100+); friends; AI-written pattern insights.

**Safety:** experiments must be safe and free/cheap. A support link is always visible. We never claim to treat depression or anxiety.

**First test (about 20 people):** >=50% finish 3 experiments; >=60% say they learned something about themselves; would they be sad if it disappeared?
